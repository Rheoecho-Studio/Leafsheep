## English

Hello, I am MIRN, the manager of RheoEcho and the lead developer of this project. Regardless of how you discovered the Boundless Leafsheep browser, I have some unfortunate news to share. As a student in China, I will be taking the National University Entrance Examination (Gaokao) in a year to gain admission to my dream university. To prevent project disorganization and to ensure I can focus fully on my exams, I have decided to suspend updates and my personal commits from **July 29, 2026, to July 31, 2027**. Rest assured, however, that all online services for Leafsheep will remain operational during this period.

During this hiatus, if you have good ideas, feel free to submit code. I will review and merge pull requests into the `rolling` branch during my free time. Additionally, I have recently been under significant pressure regarding compatibility maintenance. Despite our efforts, numerous websites have not only blocked access from Leafsheep but many APIs have also become incompatible with Leafsheep.

Therefore, I would like to announce in advance that upon my return on **July 31, 2027**, I will release a preview regarding the next generation of the Boundless browser. The next-generation browser will be a semi-independent browser—meaning we will only utilize an open-source engine, while developing the GUI and all other components ourselves. Furthermore, we will migrate away from Basilisk and its UXP platform (Goanna engine) to a different engine (Blink is currently not under consideration).
*(For example, Firefox uses the Gecko engine. If we decide to adopt Gecko, we will build all remaining components and UI around Gecko.)*

Everyone is welcome to submit naming suggestions for the next-generation Boundless browser, as well as your thoughts and ideas for it. I will also spend my leisure time brainstorming and trying to build the architecture and implementation methods for the next generation. Thank you for your understanding!

**MIRN**  
*July 29, 2026 (Shanghai, UTC+8)*
